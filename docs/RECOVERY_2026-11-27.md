## Fresh visual evaluation
Inspected the approved turnaround, six recovered anchors, existing browser contact sheet and all 56 interpolated sprites on eight enlarged transition boards. Whole-body source silhouettes are connected. The higher native resolution is useful, but the current performance fails the artistic gate:

- Stand/bend and reverse transitions visibly dissolve the scarf, arm and bag textures.
- Bend/seat changes foot support and raises both soles while compressing the torso.
- Seat/lower blends open mouth into closed mouth and duplicates lid/eye contours.
- Lower/sleep blends satchel, scarf, muzzle and overlapping limbs.
- Sleep/wake duplicates lid and jaw lines; the head does not lift as a stable volume.
- Wake/seat returns the broad smile too early, retracts support arms and raises feet.
- Fixed bottom alpha row is silhouette registration, not proof of planted feet.
- Six poses do not separately author anticipation, settling, breathing, waking stir and supported standing recovery. Sleep is a static hold.

Decision: retain v0.7.2 as an unapproved recovered study. Do not promote interpolation as the solution. Whole-body sprites remain viable for authored animation; these six anchors are insufficient for the required continuous performance. Next experiment must author smaller pose gaps, keep neutral/sleepy closed-mouth recovery, preserve contact landmarks, and reject morphing that damages faces or clothing. No speech/films.
