# DINO-001 — Character Bible

Approved explorer version (Nov 24, 2026): smooth green bipedal dinosaur, cream muzzle/belly and underside of tapering tail; large glossy amber eyes, rounded head, friendly smile, two nostrils, short arms, sturdy legs, three rounded brown nails per visible front-facing foot. Red neckerchief with front knot and two ends, triangular back flap. Brown crossbody satchel on anatomical right hip, strap over left shoulder, rectangular golden buckle and round golden flap fastener. No hair, scales, crests or spikes. Accessories ideally separate and removable in future rig; currently baked in artwork.

Personality: curious, playful, friendly, brave, observant, occasionally impulsive; expressive without requiring dialogue. Target tone combines cute humor and epic/mysterious exploration. Exact physical measurements remain unapproved; head height estimated 35–40% total standing height, not the unreliable generated 4.5-head label.

Authority order: exact selected hero and approved turnaround, then cropped views derived from turnaround, then secondary design/expression/movement artwork. Secondary sheets sometimes place bag on wrong side or change proportions/brows/teeth; do not use those variations as canon. Colors in identity JSON are provisional material swatches, not exact physical lighting-independent measurements.

Character likeness is the priority. Existing procedural GLBs were not accepted. The image-based puppet uses approved pixels but still resamples them when deforming. Do not introduce an alternate identity when adding motion.
