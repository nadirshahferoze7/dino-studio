# Make room for everyone

Hey, friends. Our time together is too precious to spend making someone feel small. Making fun of someone can hurt, even when we call it a joke.

If someone feels hurt, let's stop and listen. We all make mistakes. What matters is what we choose next.

We can say sorry, make things right, and help someone feel included. It's never too late to choose kindness. You matter. They matter too. Let's make room for everyone.

Direction: warm, clear and welcoming. Address hurtful behaviour without labelling anyone bad. Pause to listen, repair harm, and include others. These are delivery intentions, not model emotion controls.
