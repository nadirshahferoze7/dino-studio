How these variants were made

Fenrir is unchanged. The alternatives use75%Fenrir/25%Puck and70%Fenrir/30%Michael voice-style embeddings. Every line was freshly synthesized by HeadTTS/Kokoro on a production CPU. These are genuine neural variations, not pitch-shifted or mixed audio.

The blend recipes explore possible personality differences; they are not emotion controls. Every voice uses identical dialogue at speed1. No acting instructions or emotional conditioning are passed to the model. No agent listening assessment or invented artistic scores. One full-scale sample was detected in the Fenrir + Puck jolly take; its audible effect has not been assessed.

This page only plays saved WAV/MP3/MP4 and mouth timing. No AI model loads or runs on your phone. Timing comes from predicted token durations, not acoustic forced alignment. The retained speaking artwork uses eight coarse mouth poses and a still body; it does not act out sadness or joy with new facial expressions.
