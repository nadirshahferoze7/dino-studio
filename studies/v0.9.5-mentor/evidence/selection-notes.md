# Controlled mentor-range casting

Owner listened to v0.9.4 and preferred Fenrir to Puck, while liking both. No permanent selection. This supersedes Puck's prior provisional review-order lead.

Five approved test lines cover guidance, advice, reassurance, encouragement and sadness. Original Fenrir is the reference. Two style-vector variants retain a Fenrir majority:75%Fenrir/25%Puck and70%Fenrir/30%Michael. The proposed playful/grounded purposes are hypotheses, not auditory findings. No adjective is an established sonic assessment.

Kokoro's official pipeline supports blending voice embeddings by mean; the production script uses deterministic weighted float32 interpolation for all token-length vectors. Each line is then synthesized anew by the same timestamped neural model. This is not mixed recordings or pitch shifting. Upstream reference: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/blob/main/kokoro/pipeline.py (load_voice).

Official voice documentation lists Fenrir, Puck and Michael with the same target-quality/training-duration grade (B/H-hours, overallC+). This supports choosing a comparable source embedding, not an artistic ranking. https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md (retrieved2026-11-28). No additional casting sweep or emotion-engine experiments.

All three use speed1, identical text and no pitch/effect processing. Emotion intentions are not conditioning labels.
