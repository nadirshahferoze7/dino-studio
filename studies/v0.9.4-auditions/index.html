Production notes & honest limitations

Actual HeadTTS / Kokoro recordings, generated on a studio CPU. This page only plays saved media and mouth cues; it never downloads or runs an AI model.

All voices use the same words and speed, with no pitch effects. Emotion labels describe the dialogue intention; this model has no acting-intensity control. Mouth motion uses model-predicted phoneme durations and eight existing mouth poses, with a still body. The retained speech artwork is a development study, not a replacement for Dino’s approved identity.

Technical checks cover audio decoding, duration, timing bounds and rendered recovery. Puck’s Curious take contains three full-scale samples; their audible effect has not been assessed. Original engine WAVs are retained; downloadable masters correct a four-byte RIFF header size error without changing audio samples.

No auditory quality scores have been invented. Start with Puck as the provisional casting candidate, then compare Fenrir and Liam; a permanent-voice recommendation requires listening and creative approval.
