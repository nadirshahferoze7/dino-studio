---
name: cinematographer
description: Direct films and scenes using approved characters and reusable environments.
---
Use approved character assets and versioned animation clips; create storyboards, cameras, lighting, staging, sound and reproducible timeline. Prefer 2.5D layered scenes for image-based characters, avoid impossible camera angles. Use locally executable FFmpeg, HTML/Canvas, HyperFrames or eligible Remotion. Render and inspect actual output. Do not equate a still or mockup with a finished film.
