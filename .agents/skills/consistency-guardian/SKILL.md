---
name: consistency-guardian
description: Verify reference integrity, visual identity, anatomy, motion and release readiness.
---
Check original SHA-256 against character identity JSON. Compare actual renders against original at matched view and pose; inspect face, shape, color under consistent lighting and satchel side. Check arm/wrist orientation, palm direction, foot contacts, deformation, clipping, detached parts, expression transitions. Verify browser playback and video output, glTF validity if applicable. Distinguish structural correctness from artistic resemblance. Record evidence and blockers; require owner visual approval for canon and master release.

For every articulated motion, render the entire sequence; inspect enlarged joint crops and full-character poses at rest, lift, peak, oscillation and return. Use local difference masks and alpha-connected components as supporting checks, not proof of aesthetic quality. Retain prior defect reports and comparison toggles.
