name: character-designer
description: Maintain approved character identity when designing poses, new references, and characters.
---
Read character Bible and identity JSON. Use original reference assets; verify hashes; compare head, muzzle, eyes, silhouette, materials, limbs and accessory orientation. Never mirror asymmetric outfit. Separate canon from proposed design. Preserve original images. Generate only necessary additional poses or hidden parts, label them candidates, and obtain owner approval before replacing canon. Deliver reference comparisons and change notes.
