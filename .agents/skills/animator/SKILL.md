Use existing character assets; define poses, pivots, timing and easing. Separate shoulder, elbow, wrist, palm and fingers; for greeting, palm faces recipient, not knuckles/nail tips. Inspect anticipation, arc, recovery, loops, foot contacts, collisions and seams. Test front/profile as appropriate. Keep facial identity stable. Use CPU/local rendering and reusable clip manifests. Save comparison frames and playable previews; label untested or approximate motion.

For DINO-001 image cutouts, inspect arm-body joins at full raise and during lift/lower. Reconstruct the local contour and shading rather than hiding the joint with an opaque patch. Keep the previous clip and compare at identical frame indices. Do not describe a local image repair as a full rig.
